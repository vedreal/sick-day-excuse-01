## The entry

> The lift broke with me inside it, so technically I am already at work, just not on the right floor.

One line, 99 characters: the funniest sick-day excuse that lands in a single breath. The sender is not dodging work at all — they are trapped in the lift, and the only thing wrong with their attendance is the floor.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`, validating the kit's gate first: UTF-8 text of 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
